# 🌌 About Omega (Acerca de Omega) — v1.0.0

**Omega** is a comprehensive, production-grade vitality and breathwork hub inspired by the **Wim Hof Method**, sacred geometry, and modern design principles. Built with React 19, Capacitor 8, and Electron, Omega operates 100% offline with zero external network dependencies or telemetry, available across Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux.

---

## 💎 Core Pillars & Capabilities

1. **🌬️ Conscious Breathing (Bubble-Breathing)**
   - Rhythmic hyperventilation guided by multi-layer concentric rounded hexagon ripples.
   - Open retention stopwatch with real-time Personal Best detection and celebratory haptics.
   - Customizable recovery breath hold (10s to 30s) and multidimensional somatic sensation check-ins.
   - Interactive round-by-round retention bar chart with detailed breakdown.

2. **🧊 Cold Exposure & Contrast Therapy (HydroCycle)**
   - Progressive 20-Day Cold Shower Challenge spanning 4 weeks (15s, 30s, 45s, 60s).
   - Water temperature logging with seamless °C and °F conversions.
   - Calming breath pacing (4s inhale / 6s exhale) to naturally modulate thermal shock.
   - Blind shower acoustic mode guided by 432Hz harmonic chimes and preparation buffers.

3. **💪 Apnea Push-Up Endurance Test**
   - Push-up counter on empty-lung retention with zero-drift monotonic timestamp timers.
   - Historical tracking integrated into the unified vitality store.

4. **📊 Offline Vitality & Progress Tracking**
   - 28-day calendar heatmap with month range display and monthly adherence metrics.
   - 10 collectible hexagonal achievement badges with live progress bars and metallic bevels.
   - 100% private, client-side encrypted JSON backup export and import.

5. **🔷 Sacred Geometry & 432Hz Zen Audio Engine**
   - Mathematical rounded hexagon geometry (`R=86, cr=14`) with continuous perimeter progress rings.
   - Web Audio synthesizer generating authentic 432Hz Tibetan singing bowls and meditation chimes.
   - Native tactile haptic feedback on supported mobile devices.

6. **🌍 Multilingual Accessibility (7 Languages)**
   - Full localization into English (en), Spanish (es), French (fr), German (de), Italian (it), Portuguese (pt), and Simplified Chinese (zh).

## 📱 Multiplatform Support

- **Android**: Pre-compiled APK via Capacitor 8 with high-refresh rate displays (90Hz/120Hz), safe-area insets, and screen wake lock.
- **macOS**: Native DMG installer for Apple Silicon and Intel architecture packaged with Electron.
- **Windows**: Modern MSI installer optimized for touch screens, high-DPI displays, and desktop precision.
- **Linux**: Universal AppImage standalone portable binary with zero external runtime dependencies.
- **Web**: Progressive Web App (PWA) with offline caching and responsive desktop layout.
