# Omega v1.0.0 — Official Multiplatform Release

We are excited to announce the first official production release of **Omega (v1.0.0)**, an open-source, privacy-first vitality and breathwork hub inspired by the Wim Hof Method, sacred geometry, and 432Hz harmonic acoustic principles.

This milestone introduces full native multiplatform availability with dedicated automated builds for **Android** (Capacitor 8 APK), **macOS** (Universal DMG), **Windows** (MSI installer), and **Linux** (standalone AppImage). Designed strictly offline-first with zero telemetry and no tracking, all user sessions and personal records remain private on your device with complete local JSON backup and restore capabilities.

### What is New in v1.0.0:
- **Conscious Breathwork Suite**: Rhythmic breathing with multi-layer concentric ripple visualizers, open retention apnea stopwatch, and automated personal best celebration.
- **Cold Exposure & Contrast Protocols**: Progressive 20-day cold challenge, water temperature tracking in Celsius and Fahrenheit, and 432Hz acoustic guidance.
- **Endurance Testing & Gamification**: Apnea push-up endurance counter, 28-day activity heatmap, and 10 collectible hexagonal achievement badges.
- **Global Accessibility**: Complete multilingual support across English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Simplified Chinese.

Experience a distraction-free, zero-lag biohacking companion built for resilience and mindfulness across all your devices.
